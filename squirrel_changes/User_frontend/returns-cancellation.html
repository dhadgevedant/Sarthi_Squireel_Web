Returns & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: January 2025

Cancellations

You may cancel your order within 12 hours of placing it, provided it has not yet been dispatched. To cancel, contact us at connect@squirrelnuts.in or +91 70833 73681 with your order ID.

Orders that have already been dispatched cannot be cancelled. Please refer to the returns process below once you receive the item.

Returns

We accept returns only in the following situations:

  • You received a wrong item (different from what you ordered).
  • The product arrived damaged or defective.
  • The product is past its best-before date at the time of delivery.

As our products are perishable food items, we do not accept returns for change of mind or taste preference.

How to Raise a Return Request

  1. Email us at connect@squirrelnuts.in within 48 hours of delivery.
  2. Include your order ID, a brief description of the issue, and clear photos of the product and packaging.
  3. Our team will review and respond within 2 business days.

Refunds

Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 business days back to your original payment method. UPI and wallet refunds may reflect sooner.

For any questions, please contact us.
