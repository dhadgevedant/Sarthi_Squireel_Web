Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

Information We Collect

When you place an order or create an account with Squirrel Nuts, we collect:

  • Name, email address, phone number, and delivery address.
  • Payment information (processed securely by our payment gateway — we do not store card details).
  • Order history and browsing activity on our website.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process and fulfill your orders.
  • To send order confirmations, shipping updates, and support communications.
  • To improve our website and product offerings.
  • To send promotional emails if you have opted in (you can unsubscribe at any time).

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We share data only with:

  • Courier partners — to fulfill deliveries (name, address, phone).
  • Payment processors — to process transactions securely.
  • Service providers — who help us operate our website (subject to confidentiality agreements).

Cookies

We use essential cookies to maintain your shopping cart and session. We may use analytics cookies to understand how visitors use our site. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect site functionality.

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal data. However, no transmission over the internet is 100% secure. Please contact us immediately if you suspect unauthorized access to your account.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at connect@squirrelnuts.in.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated date at the top of this page reflects the latest revision. Continued use of our website after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
