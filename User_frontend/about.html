We don't make healthy snacks. We make everyday snacks healthier.

Why Choose Squirrel Nuts?

We started Squirrel Nuts with one simple belief: people shouldn't have to choose between health and taste.

Unlike conventional bakery products, our recipes are made without maida, refined sugar or palm oil. We use thoughtfully selected ingredients like whole wheat flour and corn flour to create snacks that fit naturally into your lifestyle — nutrition, freshness and flavour without compromise.
