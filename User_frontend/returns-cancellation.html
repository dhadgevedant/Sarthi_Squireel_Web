Returns & Cancellation

Our policy for cancellations, returns and refunds.

Cancellation +

Orders can be cancelled within 24 hours of placing them. After that, cancellation is not guaranteed because we start baking fresh batches quickly.

Returns +

Because our products are perishable and handmade, we do not accept returns once delivered. If you receive a damaged or wrong item, please contact us within 24 hours with photos.

Refunds +

Refunds are approved only for confirmed quality issues and are processed within 7-10 business days after verification.
