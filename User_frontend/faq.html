Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you want to know about our snacks, ingredients and delivery.

What makes Squirrel Nuts different from other snack brands?+

Unlike conventional bakery products, Squirrel Nuts focuses on creating healthier alternatives using whole wheat flour, corn flour and other thoughtfully selected ingredients. Our products are made without maida, refined sugar or palm oil.

Are your products made without maida?+

Yes. Our products are made without maida and instead use wholesome flours such as whole wheat flour and corn flour.

Do your products contain refined sugar?+

No. Our recipes are made without refined sugar and use alternative sweetening ingredients based on the product recipe.

Are Squirrel Nuts products suitable for people with diabetes?+

Some products may be suitable depending on their nutritional profile, but we recommend consulting your healthcare professional and reviewing the nutritional information before consumption.

Why should I choose Squirrel Nuts?+

Because we believe healthy eating shouldn't mean compromising on taste. Every product is thoughtfully crafted using better ingredients.

Do you offer bulk or corporate orders?+

Yes! We welcome bulk, corporate, gifting and retail partnership enquiries — contact us through the website.

How long do the products stay fresh?+

Freshly baked in small batches. Each pack carries a best-before date — store cool and dry.

Where do you deliver?+

Pan India. Dispatch within 1–2 business days, delivery in 3–7 business days.

How is my shipping charge calculated?+

Shipping is calculated automatically based on the total weight of your order, and shown on the cart page before checkout. Orders above ₹499 ship free.

What payment methods do you accept?+

Cash on Delivery is available on every order. Online payments are coming soon.

Can I cancel or return an order?+

Since our snacks are freshly baked in small batches, we're unable to accept returns once an order has shipped. If something's wrong with your order, reach out to us right away and we'll make it right.
